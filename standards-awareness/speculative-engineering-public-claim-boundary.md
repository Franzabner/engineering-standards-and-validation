# Speculative Engineering Public Claim Boundary

Status: scaffolded
Publication state: public scaffold
Release state: not released

## Purpose

This boundary keeps speculative engineering notes in hypothesis and simulation-boundary posture.

## Allowed Claims

Allowed public claims:

- a hypothesis taxonomy exists;
- a simulation-boundary checklist is scaffolded;
- examples are synthetic or public-safe;
- no public result is claimed.

## Forbidden Claims

Do not claim breakthrough results, benchmark results, measured results, proof completion, validated simulation, physical validation, proprietary method success, deployment, production readiness, customer outcome, or product readiness.

Do not include raw traces, private measurements, private workloads, customer data, endpoints, topology, credentials, or sealed source.

## Review Result

If wording turns a hypothesis into evidence, rewrite it as a question or remove it from public scope.

# AI-Assisted Engineering Claim Boundary

Status: scaffolded
Publication state: public scaffold
Release state: not released

## Purpose

This boundary keeps AI-assisted engineering documentation accurate, reviewable, and subordinate to human authority.

## Allowed Claims

Allowed public claims:

- a template exists;
- a review gate is scaffolded;
- synthetic examples demonstrate documentation structure;
- human review is required before publication, release, or architecture decisions.

## Forbidden Claims

Do not claim active agent workforce operation, autonomous production agents, live automation, production workflows, released software, deployment, production readiness, customer delivery, benchmark results, proof completion, or AI authority over human architectural decisions.

Do not include private prompts, private source paths, private logs, tool-permission maps, credentials, live tool tokens, private URLs, endpoints, or customer data.

## Review Result

If a claim cannot be supported without private workflow context, label it held for review or remove it.

# Energy Output Claim Boundary

Status: scaffolded
Publication state: public scaffold
Release state: not released

## Purpose

This boundary prevents public energy-study documentation from implying proven output, safety approval, certification, deployment, or production readiness.

## Allowed Claims

Allowed public claims:

- a no-output study boundary exists;
- a synthetic control-ledger structure is scaffolded;
- validation planning fields exist;
- no public output value is claimed.

## Forbidden Claims

Do not claim energy output values, proven output, certified safety, commercial readiness, deployed energy systems, live operations, deployment, production readiness, benchmark results, result claims, proof completion, customer operation, or safety approval.

Do not include private measurements, production CAD, production schematics, BOMs, Gerbers, exact dimensions, private sites, private topology, private source paths, or real production logs.

## Review Result

If a public note includes a value, result, or production detail, hold it for review and remove it from public migration.

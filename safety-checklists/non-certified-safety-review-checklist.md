# Non-Certified Safety Review Checklist

Status: scaffolded

| Check | Review prompt | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Boundary named | Does the artifact state what is public, private, and sealed? | planned |
| Assumptions listed | Are assumptions synthetic and reviewable? | planned |
| Risk register linked | Are risks tracked without private incident detail? | planned |
| Safety language bounded | Does the artifact avoid safety approval language? | planned |
| Release language bounded | Does the artifact avoid released or published claims? | planned |

This checklist is a review aid only and does not certify safety.
